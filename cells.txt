Your body is made of cells. A cell is so small that you need a
microscope to see it. There are many kinds of cells in your body.
Skin cells keep you safe from germs. Blood cells carry air to every
part of you, and nerve cells send messages to your brain.

Each cell is like a tiny bag of water and food. It also has a center
called the nucleus. The nucleus is the boss of the cell, because it
tells the cell what to do. When a cell gets old, it splits in two.
Then there are two new cells. That is how you grow!
