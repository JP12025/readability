The genome of an organism comprises the complete set of its hereditary
information, encoded in the nucleotide sequence of its deoxyribonucleic
acid. Although every somatic cell contains essentially identical genetic
material, cellular differentiation produces remarkably diverse tissues,
because transcriptional regulation determines which genes are expressed
in particular circumstances.

Consequently, understanding gene expression requires integrating
experimental measurements with computational analyses. Bioinformatics
provides the algorithms, statistical methods and databases necessary for
interpreting sequencing data, predicting protein structures and
reconstructing evolutionary relationships between species.
